A Wrinkle in Time – Grade 10 Literature Study

Project Overview

Welcome to the Grade 10 Literature Study of A Wrinkle in Time by Madeleine L’Engle.

This project is designed for Grade 10 international school students. Through reading, discussion, vocabulary development, and analytical writing, students will explore the novel's major themes, characters, conflicts, and literary techniques.

The course is organized into four 90-minute lessons, with each lesson covering three chapters.

Learning Objectives

By the end of this unit, students will be able to:

- Understand the plot, characters, and major conflicts in the novel.
- Identify and analyze important literary themes and symbols.
- Develop vocabulary through contextual reading.
- Support interpretations with textual evidence.
- Participate in meaningful literary discussions.
- Write clear analytical and reflective responses.

Course Structure

Lesson| Chapters| Main Theme
Lesson 1| Chapters 1–3| Belonging, Identity & the Call to Adventure
Lesson 2| Chapters 4–6| Darkness, Courage & the Shape of Space
Lesson 3| Chapters 7–9| Conformity, Control & the Loss of Self
Lesson 4| Chapters 10–12| Love, Individuality & the Power of Connection

90-Minute Lesson Format

Each lesson follows this general structure:

Time| Activity
0–10 min| Warm-up and Quickwrite
10–25 min| Chapter Review and Reading Check
25–40 min| Vocabulary in Context
40–60 min| Close Reading and Textual Evidence
60–75 min| Class Discussion
75–90 min| Writing Practice and Exit Ticket

Student Study Instructions

Before each lesson:

1. Read the assigned three chapters carefully.
2. Review the vocabulary in the workbook.
3. Think about the main characters, conflicts, and important events.
4. Bring your book and study materials to class.

During each lesson:

1. Follow the presentation slides.
2. Participate actively in discussions.
3. Support your ideas with evidence from the novel.
4. Complete the writing activities.

After each lesson:

1. Review the main ideas and vocabulary.
2. Revise your written responses.
3. Prepare for the next three chapters.

Important Reminder

Students should use their own copy of A Wrinkle in Time as the primary reading text. The presentations and workbook are supplementary learning materials and do not replace reading the novel.
